Biography

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Computational and Data Sciences at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, where I joined the joint M.Tech–Ph.D. programme in August 2023. I work with Dr. Ratikanta Behera on quantum machine learning for scientific computing.

My background is in mathematics: a B.Sc. (Honours) at Kirori Mal College, University of Delhi, followed by an M.Sc. at IIT Kharagpur. That training shapes how I work. I start from the mathematical formulation of a problem, develop an algorithm for it, implement it, and then use numerical experiments to find out what it does and does not achieve.

At IISc I have worked on physics-informed quantum neural networks, first combining them with wavelet bases for multiscale PDEs and then extending them to integro-differential and fractional equations. I am particularly interested in where quantum and classical methods differ: their mathematical properties, what they need computationally, and where each one falls short. I am now exploring quantum neural operators built around quantum wavelet transforms.

I have also assisted with teaching quantum computing and tensor computations, and I enjoy explaining mathematical ideas through code that actually runs.

Short bio for introductions

Deepak Gupta is a PhD researcher in the Department of Computational and Data Sciences at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, advised by Dr. Ratikanta Behera. He works on quantum machine learning for scientific computing, in particular physics-informed quantum neural networks for multiscale, integro-differential and fractional PDEs, and on quantum wavelet transforms. He holds an M.Sc. in Mathematics from IIT Kharagpur and a B.Sc. (Honours) in Mathematics from the University of Delhi.
