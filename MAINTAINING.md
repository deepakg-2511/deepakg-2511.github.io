# Maintaining the site

Everything is plain HTML. Edit a file, commit, push; GitHub Pages republishes within a minute or two. Preview locally first with `python3 -m http.server 8000`.

The header and footer are repeated in each page (`index.html`, `research/index.html`, `software/index.html`, `about/index.html`, `404.html`). If you change one, change all five; a project-wide search and replace in your editor does it in one step.

## Status marks

Every paper and research line carries a status, shown as a small square before the text. Use the class that matches:

| Class | Square | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| `status status--published` | filled with an outer ring | Journal or conference version |
| `status status--preprint` | filled | Public preprint (arXiv) |
| `status status--review` | half filled | Manuscript under review, not public |
| `status status--exploring` | outline | A direction without results yet |

If you start using `status--published`, add it to the legend lists on the home and research pages too (search for `aria-label="How status is marked"`).

## Add a paper

A new paper appears in two places.

**1. Home page** (`index.html`, the `<ol class="pubs">` list). Newest first:

```html
<li class="pub">
  <span class="status status--preprint">arXiv preprint, March 2027</span>
  <h3 class="pub-title"><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/XXXX.XXXXX">Paper title</a></h3>
  <p class="pub-authors"><span class="me">Deepak Gupta</span>, Co-author One, Ratikanta Behera</p>
  <p class="pub-summary">One or two sentences: the problem, the idea, the main finding and its conditions.</p>
  <ul class="links"><li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/XXXX.XXXXX">arXiv</a></li><li><a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/XXXX.XXXXX">PDF</a></li><li><a href="/research/#short-id">Summary and BibTeX</a></li></ul>
</li>
```

**2. Research page** (`research/index.html`). Inside the section for the matching research line, add a block (copy an existing `<div class="paper-block" id="...">` and edit it). Give it a short `id` and add it to the "On this page" list at the top. Each block has a BibTeX box; the `id` on the `<pre>` must match the `data-copy` value of its Copy button and be unique on the page.

When a paper is published in a journal, change its status line, e.g. `<span class="status status--published">Journal of Computational Physics, 2027</span>`, add the DOI link to its `links` list and update its BibTeX to `@article`.

## When ER-WPIQNN becomes public

1. On the home page, find the entry headed “ER-WPIQNN”: change `status--review` to `status--preprint` (or `status--published`), turn the title into a link, add the author line and the arXiv/PDF links.
2. On the research page (`id="er-wpiqnn"`), do the same, replace the line “Details and results will appear here once the manuscript is public.” with a short results paragraph, and add a BibTeX box.
3. On the home page's research line “Wavelet bases in quantum PINNs”, update the second status (“Follow-up under review”).

## Add a news item

On the home page, add a row at the top of `<dl class="log">`:

```html
<div><dt>March 2027</dt><dd>What happened, in one or two sentences. <a href="...">Link</a></dd></div>
```

Keep about six items; remove the oldest when you add one. Talks also go in the Talks list at the bottom of the research page, which uses the same markup.

## Add your CV

Save the PDF as `assets/cv/Deepak-Gupta-CV.pdf` (exactly that name) and push. The “CV (PDF)” link on the home page and the “Download CV” button on the About page appear automatically; until the file exists they stay hidden, so there is never a broken link. Delete `assets/cv/README.txt` if you like.

## Add a portrait

Save a square photo (at least 400 × 400 px) as `assets/img/portrait.jpg`. It appears at the top of the About page automatically.

## Routine updates

- **Last updated:** each page's footer says “Last updated October 2026”. Search and replace it across the five pages when you update the site.
- **sitemap.xml:** update the `<lastmod>` dates of pages you changed.
- **About page:** education, honours, teaching and service are `<dl class="records">` lists; copy a row to add one.
- **Research lines:** when the quantum neural operator work produces a preprint, change its marks from `status--exploring` on the home page and research page, and rewrite the paragraph that says there are no results yet.

## Figures

- **Home-page decomposition:** defined in `assets/js/multiscale.js`. The three fields are in the `FIELDS` object at the top (formulas from the WPIQNN paper). To add a benchmark, add an entry there and a matching radio button in `index.html` (`name="ms-field"`, `value` equal to the new key).
- **Circuit diagram** (home and software pages) and **WPIQNN chart** (research page) are inline SVGs. They were generated by the scripts in `tools/`; edit the data at the top of a script, run it with Python 3, and paste the resulting SVG over the old one. Colours come from the stylesheet, so the SVGs follow the light and dark themes automatically.
- **Social preview image:** `assets/img/og-card.png` (1200 × 630). Replace it with any image of the same size if you want a different preview.

## Design notes

- Colours and type sizes are CSS custom properties at the top of `assets/css/site.css`, with a dark-theme block below them. The two data colours, blue (`--neg`) and amber (`--pos`), are the negative and positive signs of a wavelet coefficient; use them for data and status, not decoration.
- Type: TeX Gyre Pagella for reading text and headings, Instrument Sans for navigation, metadata, captions and controls, Latin Modern Math for equations (written as MathML, so no math library is needed).
- Text measure is held to about 65 characters (`--measure`); figures may be wider.
- Motion: the only automatic animation is the home figure stepping through decomposition levels once on first view, and it is skipped when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
